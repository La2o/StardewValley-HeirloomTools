# Changelog

## 1.0.0
Initial release.

- Every tool and weapon keeps a lifetime record: tiles tilled and watered, rocks broken, geodes found, trees felled, fish caught, monsters slain, grass cut and more.
- Moments with dates: rare finds, first artifact and first fish, legendary fish, milestones and upgrades.
- Records appear at the bottom of tool tooltips. Hold Left Shift to see every moment.
- Name your tools with `N`.
- Milestone messages.
- Existing saves: tools are marked "Remembered since" the day the mod was added, and your carried tools get the farm's lifetime totals.
- Generic Mod Config Menu support and console commands.
